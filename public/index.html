From a memory to a keepsake

Let’s create
your chapter.

You don’t need a finished design.
Just something worth remembering.

  1. 01

    Share what matters.

    Send us your story, a photo or a few references on Instagram. Tell us what you would love to hold onto, even if you’re still finding the words.

  2. 02

    Imagine it together.

    We explore the subject, shape, gold and details with you. Diamonds and other lab-grown gemstones can become part of the design where relevant.

  3. 03

    Give the idea its form.

    Our in-house designers refine the direction through sketches and review. Final design, technical feasibility and pricing are confirmed after designer review.

  4. 04

    Make it your commission.

    Once your commission is confirmed, you receive an invoice and payment link. Every piece is individually customized, never mass-produced.

  5. 05

    Bring the story to life.

    Crafted by hand in fine gold and champlevé enamel, with artwork hand-drawn by our artisans to bring your chosen details into the piece.
